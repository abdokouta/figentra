# Portfolio Messaging Matrix

| Brand | Category | Audience | Promise | Primary CTA | Personality |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Figentra | AI agents + enterprise technology | Enterprise leaders | Build and deploy production AI agents | Talk to Figentra | Intelligent, precise, ambitious |
| Academorix | Sports operations SaaS | Clubs, academies, schools | Run the academy in one platform | Book a demo | Energetic, organized, competitive |
| Beautilon | Care/beauty/wellness SaaS | Centers, salons, wellness operators | Run appointments, teams and customer journeys beautifully | Book a demo | Warm, premium, calm |

## Never merge these promises

Figentra:
> We build intelligent systems.

Academorix:
> We help sports organizations operate.

Beautilon:
> We help care and beauty businesses deliver better experiences and operate efficiently.

## Cross-sell relationship

The relationship should be explained as:

> **Academorix and Beautilon are products built by Figentra.**

Do not make the products look like sub-pages of a corporate SaaS dashboard.

## Corporate site proof

The Figentra website should demonstrate the company's capability through:
- the products it builds;
- engineering approach;
- AI-agent methodology;
- customer outcomes;
- team;
- trust posture.

It should not rely primarily on a list of technologies.
