Decision · F-Cursor · Signal mint · Geist 500

A mark that blinks in the rhythm you deploy from.

We ship Concept B — the F-Cursor mark, rendered in a single ink weight, with a Signal-mint cursor bar that blinks on a 1 s steps cycle. Same rhythm every developer knows from their terminal. Paired with Geist 500: the typeface Vercel released in 2024 that Cloudflare and Turbo adopted. The mark reads at 16 px favicon, inverts cleanly on dark surfaces, and costs zero external font license to ship. It encodes what our positioning already says out loud: this company is the one you deploy on.

F-Cursor — geometry

A geometric F with three orthogonal strokes on a 120-unit grid, terminating in a Signal-mint cursor bar at its top-right. The cursor blinks live on a 1 s steps cycle, encoding engineering / command prompt / ready for input into the letterform itself.

  • viewBox 0 0 120 120
  • Cap height 72 units · ~60% of canvas · y = 24 → 96
  • Stroke weight 10 units · ≈ 14% of cap · matches Geist 500's letter weight
  • Middle-bar ratio 34 / 52 ≈ 65% · classical geometric-sans proportion
  • Signal cursor 14 × 6 units at (88, 26) · 6-unit gap from top-stroke end · fill #00E5A0
  • Ink currentColor · inherits Ink on Paper, Paper on Graphite
  • Clearspace 1× stroke weight (10 units) on all sides · ≥ 16 px margin in every render context

§02

Every size the mark renders at

§03

The terminal blink

Every cycle is 1000 ms on a steps(1) timing function — hard on, hard off. The cursor is fully opaque from 0-49% of the cycle, then fully invisible from 50-100%. Same rhythm every developer's IDE ships. Slower than a heartbeat, because the mark is not a live indicator — it is a waiting-for-input prompt. prefers-reduced-motion disables the animation; the cursor stays visible.

49% → 50% · hard cut · steps(1)

§06

Sanctioned alternates

Two secondary marks reserved for surfaces where the F-Cursor doesn't fit. Concept A ships on print, email signatures under 14 px, and any static context where the blink cannot animate. Concept C ships as the section-opening seal on /governance and /trust only.

Figentra

Concept A · wordmark only

Print, static, small sizes.

Pure Geist 500 wordmark. Reserved for print (invoice header, DPA cover), email signatures below 14 px, and social profile art where the blink cannot render.

Concept C · provenance gate

Governance seals only.

An F reduced to a spine plus top stroke; the middle-bar slot is occupied by a Signal-mint check tick. Ships as the section-opening seal on the governance and trust pages. Never the primary nav mark.
