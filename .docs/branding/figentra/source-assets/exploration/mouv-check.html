Which HeroUI Pro theme actually fits Figentra?

HeroUI Pro ships four themes: default, brutalism, glass, mouve. Only default aligns with Figentra's brand doc — and even then, you override its accent.

Theme Aesthetic Accent Fit for Figentra
default Neutral, restrained, single-layer shadows, 8px radius oklch(0.62 0.20 254) — mid-blue ✓ closest fit — override accent to Signal mint #00E5A0
brutalism Heavy strokes, hard corners, high-contrast Loud primary ≈ interesting but too loud — reads YC/crypto
glass Frosted surfaces, translucency, backdrop-blur Muted ✗ contra brand doc — no backdrop-blurred overlays per §1
mouve Soft-refined, gradients, rounded, purple-tinted Warm purple oklch(0.55 0.18 300) ✗ 5 direct brand-doc conflicts (see diff above)

Verdict

"mouve" is a beautiful, cohesive design system — but it's designed for a different tone than Figentra's. It's built for refined, contemporary, soft-luxury consumer or design-forward B2B (think: Notion, Framer, Superhuman). Every one of its signature moves — gradient buttons, 24px pill corners, layered shadows, purple hue — is exactly the kind of ornament your brand doc §direction explicitly forbids.

Where this leaves you:

You want to… Then…
Stay on-brand per your written strategy Use HeroUI Pro default theme with the accent overridden to Signal mint #00E5A0. Ship Concept B in mint. Delete "mouve" from the theme options.
Adopt the mouve aesthetic wholesale Update docs/brand/04-visual-identity.md to match: allow gradients, 24px radius, layered shadows, purple palette. That's a brand-strategy pivot, not a color swap. Founding Partners' call.
Cherry-pick just the mauve accent, keep the rest Ship Concept B with the dot in oklch(0.55 0.18 300) instead of mint. Keep flat surfaces, 4-8px radii, single-layer shadows. You get 1 conflict resolved and 5 still in place — not recommended.

My read: The mouve theme's visual language and Figentra's positioning are aiming at different customers. Figentra's ICP is a Fortune 500 CTO who wants to see "engineering discipline, governance, receipts." Mouve's visual language says "refined product, considered aesthetic, premium consumer." Both are valid design targets — but you can't be both at once, and you already wrote your answer in docs/brand/00-foundation.md §1.

Stick with Signal mint. Use HeroUI Pro's default theme as the substrate. Ship Concept B unchanged.
