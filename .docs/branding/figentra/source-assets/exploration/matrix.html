My honest read

Axis Options considered Recommendation Because
Accent color Signal mint · HeroUI mauve · current purple · mono Signal mint #00E5A0 Only color that reads as "live / running / in-production." Mauve/purple reads consumer-creative — wrong signal for CTO buyers. Mono works but leaves the deploy-heartbeat story on the table.
Font family Inter · Plus Jakarta Sans · Space Grotesk · Söhne Inter Display (with Söhne as the paid endgame) Inter is the workhorse for this exact tier. Jakarta reads too consumer; Grotesk reads too crypto. Söhne is what to migrate to once ARR justifies the $1k/yr license.
Wordmark weight 400 · 500 · 600 · 700 500 (Medium) What the brand doc §2 specifies. 400 reads editorial, 600 reads confident-but-loud, 700 reads startup/consumer. 500 is where Anthropic + Vanta + Linear live.
Mark stroke 6px · 10px · 14px 10px Holds at 16px favicon. Ratio matches Inter Display 500's letter weight. 6px disappears at small size; 14px reads brutalist.
Aesthetic direction Bold · medium · soft Medium/tight "Bold" reads YC-startup. "Soft" reads consumer. Figentra needs to read like Stripe/Anthropic — restrained confidence, no shouting, no whispering.

Where I could be wrong: if you're moving Figentra into a market where the audience is closer to product managers than CTOs, or if the Arabic customer base is your primary landing audience, Plus Jakarta Sans becomes a much stronger call for its Arabic pairing. Same story on color — if Figentra's future products aim at creative/agency verticals, mauve holds. For the currently-written "Fortune 500 CTO" positioning in docs/brand/00-foundation.md §1, though, everything above is the pocket.
