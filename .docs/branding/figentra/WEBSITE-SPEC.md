# Figentra — Enterprise Website Specification

## Objective

Move the website from a developer/operator showcase to an enterprise AI company site that clearly communicates:

1. what Figentra is;
2. what AI-agent capability it owns;
3. what business outcomes it delivers;
4. why enterprises can trust it;
5. what products it operates;
6. how its engineering model works;
7. how to start a conversation.

## Homepage layout

### 1. Hero

**Eyebrow:** `AI AGENTS · ENTERPRISE SOFTWARE · MENA → GLOBAL`

**Headline:**
> **We build AI agents that move businesses forward.**

**Subhead:**
> Figentra is an AI agents development company building intelligent software, automation and enterprise platforms for organizations ready to operate with AI.

CTAs:
- `Talk to Figentra`
- `Explore our products`

Visual:
- a live agent orchestration diagram, not a generic robot;
- human approval node;
- tools;
- systems;
- outcome state.

### 2. Outcome strip

Four outcomes:
- Automate complex work
- Ship intelligent products faster
- Connect AI to real operations
- Govern every critical action

### 3. Why Figentra

Three cards:
- **Agents built for production**
- **Enterprise engineering discipline**
- **MENA context, global ambition**

### 4. Agent development

Show lifecycle:

```text
Understand → Design → Build → Evaluate → Govern → Deploy → Observe → Improve
```

### 5. Product portfolio

Two product-led cards:
- Academorix — sports organizations
- Beautilon — health, care and beauty

Separate design language from corporate Figentra.

### 6. How we build

Explain the AI-native engineering model without exposing internal secrets.

### 7. Enterprise trust

Security, tenant isolation, observability, governance, auditability, deployment options and support.

### 8. MENA

A region-aware section:
> Built close to the markets we understand. Designed for organizations operating at scale.

Do not use generic desert/city stock imagery. Prefer real regional customer/office/industry photography when available.

### 9. Team

Leadership + selected team members with approved bios.

### 10. CTA

> **Have a business process that should be intelligent?**

`Talk to Figentra`

## Page design principle

No one-size-fits-all landing page.

- `/ai-agents`: technical systems visual language.
- `/products/academorix`: energetic sports operations visual language.
- `/products/beautilon`: premium care/wellness visual language.
- `/security`: restrained trust-center layout.
- `/team`: editorial people-first layout.
- `/customers`: evidence-first case-study layout.

## Trust content

Required before production launch:
- security overview;
- vulnerability reporting;
- privacy;
- terms;
- data processing position where applicable;
- uptime/status destination;
- customer support route;
- responsible AI position.

## SEO baseline

Every page requires:
- unique title;
- unique meta description;
- canonical URL;
- Open Graph image;
- Twitter/X card;
- structured data where appropriate;
- sitemap inclusion;
- robots policy;
- accessible headings;
- descriptive image alt text.

## Asset requirements

Every major marketing page gets:
- hero visual;
- supporting illustration/diagram;
- OG image;
- mobile crop;
- dark/light variant where needed.

Vector-first for diagrams and marks. Photography for people and proof. Product UI screenshots must be real product captures.
