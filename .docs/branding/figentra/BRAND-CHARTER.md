# Figentra — Brand Charter

## Positioning

**Figentra is an AI agents development company building production-grade
AI-native software for enterprises in MENA and global markets.**

Figentra is not positioned primarily as a generic SaaS vendor or conventional
software consultancy. Its differentiation is the combination of:

- AI agents;
- governed agentic engineering;
- enterprise software;
- automation;
- reusable platform capabilities;
- MENA-rooted execution with global ambition.

## Brand architecture

```text
Figentra Technologies LLC
        │
        ├── Figentra
        │   AI agents + enterprise technology
        │
        ├── Academorix
        │   Sports operations SaaS
        │
        └── Beautilon
            Health, care & beauty SaaS
```

## Audience

Primary:

- enterprise technology leaders;
- CIO / CTO / CDO;
- transformation leaders;
- operations leaders;
- founders and technology companies;
- MENA enterprises and institutions.

Secondary:

- developers and technical talent;
- strategic partners;
- investors;
- ecosystem partners.

## Messaging

### Master message

> **Build with AI agents. Operate with intelligence.**

### Supporting message

> Figentra builds production-grade AI agents and the enterprise systems around
> them — from engineering automation to intelligent operational software.

### MENA position

> **Built in MENA. Designed for the world.**

This is a positioning statement, not a claim of geographic exclusivity.

## Voice

Confident, technical, precise, ambitious and human.

Avoid:

- empty AI hype;
- “revolutionary” without evidence;
- fake customer proof;
- unexplained acronyms;
- generic “digital transformation” language;
- claiming autonomous capability where a human approval is required.

## Visual direction

Retain the established Figentra F-Cursor and Signal Mint system as the
foundation, but evolve the expression from a developer/operator-only brand into
an enterprise AI company.

The brand should feel:

- intelligent;
- precise;
- technical;
- premium;
- calm;
- trustworthy;
- MENA-rooted without relying on stereotypical regional imagery.

## Logo and favicon rule

The **same canonical primary mark** must be used across:

- website favicon;
- browser tabs;
- app icons;
- social avatar;
- OG image mark;
- documentation chrome;
- product references.

Do not maintain competing first-screen and second-screen icon variants.

The favicon source must be the canonical vector mark, with generated raster
exports from that source.

## Illustration system

Figentra illustrations should explain systems, agents, workflows and outcomes.

Preferred subjects:

- agent orchestration;
- connected systems;
- workflow graphs;
- terminal/code environments;
- enterprise operations;
- intelligent automation.

Use the existing terminal-native visual language where it strengthens technical
credibility, but introduce outcome-oriented diagrams and real enterprise
contexts for marketing.

## Photography

Use real team/customer-approved photography for:

- leadership;
- team;
- company story;
- events;
- customer stories.

Do not use generic stock people to represent Figentra employees or customers.

## Color

Existing Signal Mint `#00E5A0` remains the primary accent.

The enterprise system should use neutral ink/paper foundations with semantic
supporting colors for status and data visualization.

## Typography

Use Geist + Geist Mono consistently with the established system.

## Accessibility

- WCAG 2.2 AA.
- Keyboard-complete navigation.
- Visible focus.
- Reduced-motion support.
- Text alternatives for meaningful imagery.
- Decorative imagery must not become semantic noise.

## Brand boundary

Figentra owns the corporate promise and technology narrative.

Academorix and Beautilon must not be forced into the Figentra visual language.
