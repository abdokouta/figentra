# Figentra Brand Architecture

**Status:** CANONICAL / APPROVED
**Date:** 2026-09-04

This document defines the commercial and product naming architecture for Figentra. It is the naming authority for company, platform, agentic-development technology, and application products.

## 1. Executive decision

Figentra has four distinct levels:

```text
FIGENTRA
Company / Brand
    |
    +-- FIGENTRA AGENTIC DEVELOPMENT
    |   AI-native software development platform
    |
    +-- FIGENTRA OS
    |   Enterprise operating platform
    |
    +-- FIGENTRA APPLICATIONS
        +-- Academorix
        +-- Beautilon
        +-- Future products
```

These names must not be used interchangeably.

## 2. Figentra — Company

**Figentra** is the company, technology brand, and ecosystem.

Recommended corporate positioning:

> **Figentra is an agentic development company pioneering AI-native software development from the Middle East and Africa for a global market.**

Figentra builds software through AI-agentic engineering, creates reusable enterprise technology, and turns that technology into products for customers.

### Primary category

**Agentic Development Company**

### Geographic identity

Figentra is **born in the Middle East and Africa (MEA)** and operates with a **global ambition and market**.

Do not constrain the company to MENA as its market. MENA can be used when specifically discussing the North Africa + Middle East region, but the canonical corporate positioning is **Middle East and Africa with global reach**.

## 3. Figentra Agentic Development — Product

**Figentra Agentic Development** is a product/platform, not the company name.

It is the AI-native system Figentra uses to plan, design, build, test, review, secure, deploy, and operate software through coordinated AI agents and human oversight.

### Positioning

> **Figentra Agentic Development is an AI-native software development platform where specialized agents collaborate across the software lifecycle to build production systems.**

### Short description

> **AI agents building production software.**

### Product role

Figentra Agentic Development is the **how we build** layer of the Figentra ecosystem.

It can consume Figentra OS capabilities and can ultimately build applications that run on Figentra OS.

## 4. Figentra OS — Product

**Figentra OS** is the enterprise operating platform.

> **Figentra OS is the operating foundation for modern enterprise software and digital businesses.**

It is the **what we build on** layer.

It provides shared enterprise capabilities such as identity, tenancy, IAM, monetization, usage, workflow, notifications, audit, files, integrations, search, reporting, analytics, marketing, developer infrastructure, runtime foundations, and experience infrastructure.

The detailed product definition is maintained in `docs/figentra-os.md`.

## 5. Applications

Figentra applications are customer-facing products and business solutions built through the Figentra ecosystem.

### Academorix

**Academorix** is a Figentra application/product.

Its business domain, product strategy, customer experience, business data, and application-specific logic remain owned by Academorix. Figentra OS supplies reusable platform capabilities.

### Beautilon

**Beautilon** is the canonical name replacing **Beauty Loan**.

Beautilon is a Figentra application/product. Its business domain and application logic remain product-owned while it can consume Figentra OS capabilities.

### Naming rule

Use **Beautilon** in all new documentation, code names, product catalogs, diagrams, presentations, and references. `Beauty Loan` is a legacy name and must not be used as the current product name.

## 7. What Figentra is

Figentra is simultaneously:

- An agentic development company
- A technology company
- A platform company
- A product company
- An ecosystem builder

The company-level identity is broader than any individual platform or application.

## 8. What Figentra is not

Do not describe Figentra as only:

- An AI coding assistant
- A software agency
- A collection of microservices
- An authentication company
- A generic SaaS company
- Figentra OS itself
- Figentra Agentic Development itself

Those are either narrower capabilities or products within the broader company.

## 9. Relationship between Agentic Development and OS

The two flagship technology products have complementary responsibilities:

```text
                   FIGENTRA
                      |
          +-----------+-----------+
          |                       |
   AGENTIC DEVELOPMENT       FIGENTRA OS
          |                       |
   Build software           Operate software
          |                       |
          +-----------+-----------+
                      |
                Applications
```

Agentic Development focuses on software creation and lifecycle execution.

Figentra OS focuses on the runtime and enterprise foundation required to operate software.

They are complementary products, not competing operating systems.

## 11. Geographic positioning

The geographic story has two layers:

### Origin

**Middle East and Africa** is the company's home and regional identity.

### Market

**Global** is the company's intended market and ambition.

Therefore:

> **Built from the Middle East and Africa. Built for the world.**

This is preferable to branding Figentra as a "MENA-only" company.

### Figentra OS

## 13. Naming rules

1. Always capitalize the brand as **Figentra**.
2. Always write **Figentra OS** for the enterprise operating platform.
3. Always write **Figentra Agentic Development** for the agentic development product.
4. Never use `Figentra Agentic Development OS` as a product name.
5. Always use **Beautilon** as the current product name.
6. Do not call Academorix or Beautilon "Figentra OS modules"; they are applications/products.
7. Do not call Figentra OS a company.
8. Do not call Figentra Agentic Development the company.
9. Use **Middle East and Africa** for corporate regional positioning unless a narrower regional statement is intentional.
10. Use **global** when describing the intended market or ambition.

## 15. Final decision

This is the canonical Figentra commercial naming model.

**Figentra is the company. Figentra Agentic Development and Figentra OS are technology products. Academorix, Beautilon, and future applications are product/application brands.**
