# Figentra Product

Figentra is an AI-native, engineering-driven technology company. `Figentra Agentic Development` is the software-development platform: coordinated specialized agents, project context, governance, tooling and quality gates used to build production software. `Figentra OS` is the enterprise runtime foundation on which resulting systems operate.

For detailed product authority read `docs/figentra-agentic-development.md`. For the engineering operating model read `docs/engineering-system/README.md`.

Do not treat product marketing language as technical authority. Product requirements and approved specifications determine implementation behavior.
